```markdown
### T32. Repair-pipeline hack (ride-the-mend)
- **Status:** experimental — design only; grows out of T31; no sessions yet
- **Premise:** the Synod auto-maintains its infrastructure. A drone disabled and left ~a day or two is collected, repaired, and returned to service — indifferently, without hunting Milo for it. This is the **loss-condition resolution for T31's captured drone** (loss has texture, not a cheap respawn: a lost drone re-enters the patrol population and must be found again among others) AND a system with a seam.
- **The hack:** if Milo can *address* a drone (T31 terminal control), he can address the **repair queue**. Leave a unit to be "repaired" and ride the repair pipeline into infrastructure otherwise unreachable. **You hack the system by letting it fix your Trojan** — the drone gets in by being a thing the network wants to mend. This is the **imitation principle (T10) applied to infrastructure instead of behavior**: acceptance by being what the system already wants to preserve.
- **The knowledge gap IS the puzzle (frozen design architecture):** Milo (character, embedded in the system) plausibly knows the pipeline exists; the *player* does not. That gap is the puzzle's architecture, delivered diegetically — **Milo never narrates knowledge the player hasn't earned** (diegetic-only-delivery rule; narrating it would steal the puzzle). Two layers:
  1. **Perception:** notice disabled drones *disappear* and *reappear intact* — infer a pipeline from environmental evidence alone (no marker, no tutorial). The Synod's self-repair is shown, never stated.
  2. **Exploitation:** realize the pipeline can be *addressed* — the conceptual leap from "the system fixes its own" to "I can be a thing it fixes." The hack is a conceptual discovery, not a mechanical unlock.
- **Closing the gap without cheating:** the player closes the gap by **catching up to Milo's system-literacy** — Milo's job-familiarity surfaces as *legible interface* (e.g. a disabled drone carries a Lineglass tag the player learns reads as "this will be collected"), not as words. Milo sees; the player learns to see what Milo sees. The discovery arc *is* the theme (system legibility vs. human ambiguity) as a puzzle-teaching curve — the outsider becoming embedded. That literacy is the reward.
- **Menace note:** the system's *patience* (heals rather than retaliates) is more unsettling than pursuit — hold that tone; no vengeful-network drift.
- **Cross-links:** T31 (parent; loss condition; terminal control) · T13 (break-and-hack) · T26/T27 (repair queue as addressable transmission target; ride-in as mediation-layer event) · T10 (imitation → infrastructure).
- **Blocked by:** all of T31's v2 (terminal control especially). Pure downstream. Owning doc: TBD.
```
