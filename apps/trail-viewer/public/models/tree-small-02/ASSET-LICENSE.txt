Runtime derivative
------------------

Generated from Poly Haven's 1K glTF with gltfpack 1.1:

  gltfpack -i tree_small_02_1k.gltf -o tree_small_02_preview.glb -si 0.025 -sp -kn -km

The pass preserves the original nine 1K JPEG textures. It reduces the model
from 2,062,487 to 52,353 triangles and packages it as one GLB for the browser
preview. This derivative is also available under CC0.

Trunk+branch diffuse (bark/wood) were further color-normalized (2026-07-23)
against the other 4 tree species' bark/wood textures — six bark/wood
diffuse maps across all species were visibly inconsistent in tone (Poly
Haven luminance ranged 0.22-0.41 across them, different photo shoots),
blended 60% toward the 6-texture average via the same NumPy/Pillow
Reinhard-style statistical color transfer used for the ground textures
(see apps/trail-viewer/public/textures/rocky-terrain-02's ASSET-LICENSE.txt
for the technique). Leaves diffuse was deliberately left untouched — it's
an alpha-cutout card atlas with a transparent background (a whole-image
tone transform would corrupt it), and different species having different
foliage color is correct, not something to normalize away.

Meshopt runtime compression is intentionally disabled for this preview so the
Babylon client does not need to fetch a decoder from an external CDN.
