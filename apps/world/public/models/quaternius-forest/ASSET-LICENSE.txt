Selection notes (this repo, 2026-08-01)
----------------------------------------

Raw multi-file glTF (mesh + .bin + loose textures), same convention as
`downtown-city-megakit/` — not baked into a single GLB like the Poly Haven
hero trees, since each species here is already a small single-mesh asset with
no benefit from gltfpack decimation.

Imported for the T7 forest-impasse/grove/secret-entrance work
(`docs/intake/world-foliage-impasse-prompt-v1.md`, executed via the live
locations.json + thin-instance pipeline rather than that doc's Blender/navmesh
assumptions — see ForestImpasses.ts):

- `BirchTree_1..5.gltf` / `MapleTree_1..5.gltf` — canopy/hero variety, used for
  the grove clearing's canopy-frame anchor trees and general impasse-ring fill.
- `DeadTree_1..10.gltf` — ten distinct silhouettes for deadfall-stack impasses
  and the grove's standing snag landmark (varied angle/decay reads without
  repeating the same trunk).
- `Bush.gltf`, `Bush_Small.gltf`, `Bush_Large.gltf` (+ `_Flowers` variants) —
  dense shrub-wall impasses and vine-tangle-style understory fill (this pack
  has no literal vine/rope geometry; dense stacked bush/deadtree clusters
  stand in for that visual, per the source doc's own "vine-heavy brush"
  framing).
- `Flower_1.gltf`, `Flower_2.gltf`, `Flower_1..5_Clump.gltf`,
  `Grass_Small.gltf`, `Grass_Large.gltf`, `Grass_Large_Extruded.gltf` — grove
  floor / ground-cover accents.

Left out deliberately: `NormalTree_*`, `PalmTree_*`, `PineTree_*`, and
`Rocks.jpg` — this particular download only includes texture maps for those,
no mesh/.gltf files, so there was nothing importable for them. (`DeadTree_*`
happens to reference the `NormalTree_Bark` texture set for its bark material —
that pairing is intentional in the source pack, not a substitution made here.)

Texture rework
--------------

`BirchTree_Bark(.jpg/_Normal.png)`, `MapleTree_Bark(.jpg/_Normal.png)`, and
`NormalTree_Bark(.jpg/_Normal.png)` (used by DeadTree) shipped at 2048x2048;
the normal maps in particular were multi-megabyte, poorly-compressed PNGs
(~19-22MB each uncompressed). Downscaled to 512x512 with ffmpeg to match this
repo's existing ground-texture convention (`rocky-terrain-02`,
`marble-cliff-03`, both `_512`-suffixed) — nothing here reads at a distance
close enough to need more resolution than the terrain textures already get:

  ffmpeg -i <name>_Bark.jpg -vf scale=512:512 -q:v 3 <name>_Bark.jpg
  ffmpeg -i <name>_Bark_Normal.png -vf scale=512:512 -compression_level 9 <name>_Bark_Normal.png

`BirchTree_Leaves.png`, `Bush_Leaves.png`, `Flowers.png`, `Grass.png`, and
`MapleTree_Leaves.png` were already 1024x1024 alpha-cutout cards and were left
untouched. `MapleTree_Leaves_BW.png` was not copied — none of the five
imported Maple variants reference it.
